About Luxe Gifts LK

Sri Lanka's premium gifting destination crafting moments of joy since 2018.

Our Story

Founded in Colombo in 2018, Luxe Gifts LK began with a simple belief: every celebration deserves something beautiful. What started as a small flower studio has grown into Sri Lanka's trusted premium gifting destination delivering handcrafted bouquets, artisan celebration cakes, and curated gift hampers to thousands of happy customers every year.

We work with local florists, bakers, and artisans to ensure every item that leaves our studio is crafted with care. From a single rose to a grand celebration hamper, every order is treated with the same attention to detail.

Our Promise

Premium Quality. Only the finest seasonal blooms, premium ingredients, and trusted gift brands make it into our collection.

Island wide Delivery. We deliver to all 25 districts in Sri Lanka with same day service in Colombo for orders placed before 2 PM.

Customer First. Every order is confirmed by phone, and our team is available 7 days a week to assist with your gifting needs.
